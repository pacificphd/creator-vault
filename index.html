FAQ

How do I receive my files after purchase?

Complete payment while signed in. After payment is verified, the purchased product appears in My Downloads inside the same Creator Vault account.

Which software do these products work with?

Compatibility depends on the product. Check the product details before buying for supported apps such as Adobe Premiere Pro, Lightroom, Photoshop, DaVinci Resolve or other listed tools.

Can I use Creator Vault assets for paid client projects?

Yes, where the product license allows client and commercial project use. You may not resell, redistribute, share or repackage the original source files as your own product.

Can I download a purchased product again later?

Yes. Eligible purchases remain connected to your account and can be accessed from My Downloads while the product remains available under its purchase terms.

Are digital product purchases refundable?

Because these are downloadable digital products, refunds are generally not offered after access or download has been provided. If you have a genuine payment or file-access issue, contact support so it can be checked.
